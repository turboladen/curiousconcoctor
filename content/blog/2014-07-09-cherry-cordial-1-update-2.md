Well, this may not be so much of a cordial anymore. It seems like by adding the yeast to the mix,
it’s fermented away quite a bit of the flavor.

Racked to my fermenter bucket with the idea that once the stuff separates again, I can spoon off the
krausen, siphon the clear, and leave the yeast, then add the vodka. In the mean time, since flavor
went byebye, I added 1/2 cup brown sugar (directly—didn’t think to dissolve it first) and 1/2 cup
maple syrup (for some reason I thought to add 1/2 cup hot water to that…) and stirred a bit. Will
try to remember to do the transfer in the morning. It’s gonna be interesting at least…
