Doing some sparking, some still:

Sparkling:

- 2 750ml champagne bottles
- 9 12oz bottles

Shooting for 2.6 volumes CO2. Only had 0.8oz priming sugar, so adding 0.9oz Kristina’s wildflower
honey, diluted in 1.5oz water (about 1/2 cold, 1/2 from the 190F hot water source), then mixed the
sugar in.

Still:

- 6 750ml wine bottles
- 4 12oz bottles (last one had about 3 oz)

---

Popped the airlock off and it still smells crazy chemical-y. Bottled it anyway! After some
post-bottling tasting, I still get a little of the throat burn and tongue tingle, but it’s
definitely not as bad as the last time I racked; still not drinkable though. In a wine glass, it has
solid legs. Color is mostly dark, cherry red, with a hint of brown.
