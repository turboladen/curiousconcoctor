## Ingredients

- 26 lbs yellow peaches, twice frozen (first time 2 weeks, second about 3 days)
- 2 navel oranges, juice
- 4 pink lady apples
- 3 vanilla beans
- 1 Ceylon cinnamon stick, whole
- 3t pectic enzyme
- 6oz date syrup
- 6oz blackstrap molasses
- Red Star Cotes des Blancs

Juiced the peaches, added all pulp. Juiced the oranges, added 0 pulp, zest from 1. Added juice and
pulp from apples. Added water up to 6 G (about 3 G). Scraped vanilla beans; added 1 husk. Added
pectic enzyme.
