## The Story

I had saved some apple juice from the 2Q carboy I bought for racking the Blackberry Pear Melomel a
couple weeks ago, and it occurred to me that I should probably be putting it to good use–and that
means, of course, concocting something new. I checked my cupboards and found that I had plenty of
honey, vanilla, spices, yeast, etc. to make this happen, thus there was now no reason for it to not
happen.

Given that a good amount of aging would put me at the holiday season for drinking, I figured I’d do
some research for recipes for inspiration and came
[across this recipe for “Apple Pie Mead”](http://www.homebrewtalk.com/f30/apple-cinnamon-mead-13109/).
I didn’t follow it to the T, but it served as a great starting point.

## Recipe

- 2 Q organic apple juice (WF 365, pasteurized)
- 2/3 stick of
  [ceylon (aka “real”) cinnamon](http://www.whfoods.com/genpage.php?tname=newtip&dbid=31), crushed
  via mortar & pestle
- 1/2 nutmeg nut, crushed via mortar & pestle
- 2 vanilla beans
- 2 handfuls raisins
- peel of 1 [sumo orange](http://www.sumocitrus.com/)
- ~1 lb. wildflower honey from Kingsburg Honey
- 1 lb. [Gipson’s Golden](http://www.gipsonsgolden.com/) wildflower honey
- 1 packet [Red Star Premier Cuvée](http://www.midwestsupplies.com/red-star-premier-cuvee.html)

I’d recently started reading
[Ken Schramm’s, _The Compleat Meadmaker_](http://www.amazon.com/The-Compleat-Meadmaker-Production-Award-winning/dp/0937381802),
and learned, amongst other things, that the “cinnamon” that we get here is usually cassia–not real
cinnamon. Real cinnamon, called “ceylon cinnamon” or “true cinnamon”, is lighter and sweeter and
provides some extra health benefits over cassia. I’d recently got some from Amazon, just in case
something like this came up.

I added the orange peel to try to ameliorate that warm, holiday, spicy feel. The honey selection was
simply because that’s all I had in my cupboard. The yeast selection, again, was simply because
that’s all I had on hand.

Extra ingredients make for a fun afternoon.

## Log

### 15:50.

Brought cinnamon and nutmeg to a boil in the muslin bag then removed from heat. Added juice and
raisins to fermenter. Added orange peel to the muslin bag and added the bag to the must. Added
honey. Used the boil water to rinse out the honey jar. Stirred for about 2m.

TODO [putting it all together]

Pitched yeast at 100F and let sit for 15m. Stirred and poured into fermenter. Stirred briskly for
about 5m. Tested OG: `1.06x`. Too low so I added 1lb of the GG honey, stirred for another 3m or so.
Tested OG: `1.084 at 79F` (adjusts to `1.085 at 68F`). ABV = `11.34%` at 1.0. pH = `4.4` (reading
the max on the strip). Didn’t airlock–just put some foil over the hole.

### 22:00.

Nice foam going on the top! Stirred for 3m.
