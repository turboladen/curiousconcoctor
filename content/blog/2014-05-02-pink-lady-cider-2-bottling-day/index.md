`FG: 0.995` (`6.96%`). Pretty dry!

Added 24g wildflower sage honey from
[Kristina’s Ranch Market](http://www.kristinasranchmarket.com/retailer/store_templates/shell_id_1.asp?storeID=BF75627F34754A0CA5145783079AF04D)
to 4oz water at 160F for each gallon. Racked into the plastic fermenter, 1G at a time. This dumped a
lot of pulp into the fermenter, which ended up clogging the siphon & hose pretty regularly.
Should’ve strained during the rack. Yielded 5 Belgian bottles and 8 12ozers.
