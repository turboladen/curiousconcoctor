I’ve got about 4 gallons of mead to bottle.

Bottles & caps/corks:

- 9 champagne 750ml, Belgian corks
- 1 Belgian 750ml, Belgian cork
- 2 750ml wine (for still), size 8. Well, meant to make still bottles but forgot to. :-/
- 4 22oz beer bottles
- 4 375ml champagne, Belgian corks
- 7 12oz beer bottles

29 bottles total.

Using 190g (~0.96 cups) corn sugar for priming. Forgot to dissolve it first–crap! After bottling, I
found probably 3/4″ deep of sugar (in the brew bucket), so I used a baby spoon and spooned spoonfuls
into each bottle, with the 750ml champagne & Belgian bottles getting 2 scoops.

Refractometer says SG is `1.041`. …WTH?
