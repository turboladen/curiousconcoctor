So I may have forgotten to take notes on bottles 2 and 3, but I thoroughly enjoyed them and can’t
wait till next year’s lemons to come around so I can do this all over again.

Drinking the last bottle tonight. Super good. Great pétillant carbonation. Good mouthfeel. Honey
notes in the fragrance (ie honey isn’t lost). Nice color. A tiny hint of bitter lemon peel on the
back end–not sure if i don’t like the bitter or if that’s a good thing for evening out the
sweetness.
