## The Story

In this household, strawberry season means thinking of anything and everything to put strawberries
in and on. …and for me this year means “I wonder what I can concoct out of them?”

My wife scored some great strawberries from a local stand and picked up some honey for me in
Kingsburg. Which reminds me that I’m annoyed at our current CSA box peoples (I won’t name names)–I’d
gotten excited when I heard they’d offer Kingsburg honey as part of the CSA box, so I, of course,
ordered away, only to have them charge me for the honey but fail to actually include the honey,
_twice_. One time = no problemz, it happens; the second time in a row though? Ugh. I digress.

My idea for this melomel is this. I want the berries to be the prominent flavor, yet not slam you in
the face. I want it sparkling–pétillant, actually–so it feels light and crisp, yet soft. I want it
dry enough that it bites, yet still sweet, although not so sweet that it feels syrupy.

And then there’s the lavender… I’m still on the fence about this. I really like the idea of the
strawberry/lavender combo, and it even seems like it fits the picture I have for this concoction. …I
just fear that adding the lavender will be too much. I’m definitely leaning towards adding it, but I
may renege on this. The fragrance of lavender is so refreshing and soothing though, that if I can
get it right, I think this could be an excellent summer beverage.

## Recipe

- 3 lbs local, fresh strawberries
- 4 lbs [Kingsburg Honey](http://www.kingsburghoney.com/) Wildflower honey
- 4 [Bartlett pears](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_pear)
- 2 grams fresh
  [lavender](http://www.livestrong.com/article/500806-what-are-the-benefits-of-eating-lavender/)
- Raisins
- Meyer lemon cubes
- 1 packet [Lalvin 71B-1122](http://www.lalvinyeast.com/71B.asp)
- 1 teaspoon [pectic enzyme](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pectinase)

## Log

### 16:23.

Washed 47 oz. berries in TJ’s fruit and veggie wash. Only cut out stems from a few; removed leaves
from all. Juiced. Yielded just less than 1L. Added about 1L water directly from the fridge–forgot to
boil. Added 3 lbs. honey. Added handful of raisins. Added boiling water to 4 lemon cubes to make
32oz. total. Added more water to get to about 6L.

### 23:50.

Juiced the pears, added juice. Added pear mash to strawberry mash in muslin bag, with marbles. OG:
1.110@78F. Total volume ~= 7L. Pitched yeast in 104F for 15 minutes in 5oz of water. Added and
stirred my butt off. Covered with lid and cheesecloth.
