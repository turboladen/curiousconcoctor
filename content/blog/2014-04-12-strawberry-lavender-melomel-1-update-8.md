`SG: 1.006`. Racked to 1G carboy, 1 750ml flip, and 1 375ml. Corked the 375ml for funsies. Still
smells like it did at the start (amazingly) lots of berry, which I think must be hiding the yeasty
smells. I think the pear gave it some roundness–maybe too much actually. Still get a glimpse of the
honey, which is just enough IMO. Deep smell gives a little extra alcohol burn in the nose; would
like that to go away. Maybe carbonation and/or age will help.
