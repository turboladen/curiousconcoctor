After letting it off-gas for a bit, I started to enjoy a bit of my sample. It’s still pretty easy,
so to speak–easy on the fruit, and easier than I was wanting–and that’s what I get for going light
on the fruit. I may want to add some more peaches to this to bring out that peach flavor. We’ll see.
Honey is certainly present in the bouquet; alcohol present in the nose. I’d been wondering if this
would need some added complexity–and I’m still not sure that it doesn’t–but I’m going to lay off for
now and keep it simple. I still plan on carbonating this and some bubbles will help that out a bit,
I think.
