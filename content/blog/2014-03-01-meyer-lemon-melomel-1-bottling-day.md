It’s time to bottle my Meyer Lemon Melomel; you can see the recipe and fermentation process
[here](http://curiousconcoctor.com/the-next-step-meyer-lemon-melomel/). This is my first experience
bottling anything, so I’m excited yet have no idea what I’m doing. I bought some
[Belgian 750ml bottles](http://www.midwestsupplies.com/belgian-750-ml-beer-bottles-case-of-12.html)
from my LHBS, so I’ll be using those and a 375ml bottle from a tasty
[Mikkeller](http://mikkeller.dk/) brew that I recently had.

## Equipment

- 3 Belgian 750ml bottles
- 1 375ml bottle
- 3 [Belgian bottle corks](http://www.midwestsupplies.com/belgian-beer-corks.html)
- 1
  [#8 straight cork](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B005IQECLO/ref=wms_ohs_product?ie=UTF8&psc=1)
- [Portuguese Double Lever Corker](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B000FRWJNE/ref=wms_ohs_product?ie=UTF8&psc=1)
- [Postassium Metabisulfate](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00838WGV2/ref=oh_details_o00_s00_i01?ie=UTF8)
- 1/4c orange blossom honey ([Gipson’s Golden](http://www.gipsonsgolden.com/))

I used the big Belgian bottles because a) I wanted to bottle condition, b) bottling in 750ml bottles
seems like the thing to do for mead. Any bottle that I use needs to be able to withstand the
pressure from the CO2 (aka carbonation) that gets created as a result of adding new sugar (honey, in
this case) to the mead, thus regular wine bottles won’t cut it here.

## Log

### 9:15am.

Been soaking the bottles in hot soapy water for about 45 mins now. Also washed corks and am soaking
in KMeta.

### 11:40am.

Dissolving honey in 8oz filtered water from fridge. Funneled honey-water to new carboy. Racked the
must to the new carboy; SG 0.997 (10.22% ABV) Filled the bottles. Tasting now. Pretty tart but
actually pretty good. A hint of offness, but really gets the taste buds going! Excited!
