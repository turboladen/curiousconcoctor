## Ingredients

- Probably 40 lemony, thinly sliced
- 8 lbs cane sugar
- Large spoonful creamed honey
- 12oz raisins
- 3t yeast nutrient
- 3t pectic enzyme
- Water to fill to 5G
- Premiere Cuvée yeast

OG 1.095
