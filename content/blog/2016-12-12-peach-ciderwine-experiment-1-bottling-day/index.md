Still the ClO2 has lots of white stuff on top; the control does not.

![The control carboy, with a clip handle on its neck, next to the ClO2 carboy, whose glass above the liquid is coated in white foam](photo-1.jpg)

![The lower halves of the two carboys on a wood floor, with sediment at the bottom of each and the ClO2 carboy in a black harness](photo-2.jpg)

![The two carboys side by side, the control cider a deeper gold than the paler ClO2 cider](photo-3.jpg)

Going to use plain ol corn sugar for both. There’s a little more overall volume to the control, but
less sediment, so I’m gonna say the control is about 2.5G, whereas the ClO2 is about 2G. I’m going
to aim for 3.3 gravities, meaning

- control: 94.19 grams
- ClO2: 75.35

Bottled:

- Control, green caps: 12 12oz bottles, 7 22oz bottles
- ClO2, orange caps: 12 12oz bottles, 4 + 1/2 22oz bottles
