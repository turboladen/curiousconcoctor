Alright, so since
[I fubared my last racking of this mead](@/blog/2014-07-20-strawberry-lavender-melomel-1-update-14.md),
I made another attempt tonight.

Once again, to sterilize the oak chips I boiled them in a small skillet (a skillet, just to heat the
water faster) for about 5 minutes then let cool for about 30 minutes. I could be wrong, but this
also seems to cook away some of the strong flavors that come from the oak–the first time I oaked, I
boiled the chips the same way, but added the boil water in addition to the chips to the must (it
smells amazing while you’re boiling the chips–it seems like that smell should accompany the must);
the end product/mead was super strong on the oak.
