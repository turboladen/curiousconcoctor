I’m super excited about what’s to come with my
[Meyer Lemon Melomel](@/blog/2013-12-the-lemons-started-it-all-sima/index.md), and that excitement
has got me curious about using different fruit in a mead. So to break away from the citrus vein, I
decided to go to the store and pick out some other fruits that might do the trick. To make my
decision, I just asked myself the simple question: what would go good with honey?

I ended up with a carton of blackberries and a couple pounds of Bartlett Pears. I still don’t really
have any good mead resources, so I sorta made up my own recipe.

## Ingredients

- 3 lbs. honey from Bob’s Bees at the
  [Vineyard Farmers Market](http://www.vineyardfarmersmarket.com/). He said it’s mostly orange
  blossom honey, but is a mixture.
- 2 lb. 12 oz. sliced [Yellow Bartlett Pears](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_pear), sliced
  and frozen
- 12 oz. Blackberries, bought fresh then frozen
- 8 oz. apple juice
- 1 vanilla bean
- 4 frozen Meyer lemon cubes
- < 1 t. bakers sugar
- 5 g. Medium toast American oak chips

I was happy to find some local honey at the farmer’s market–pretty reasonably priced too! I had to
buy another carboy to make this, since my only other one-gallon carboy was being used by the lemon
mead. Thus, another gallon of apple juice (for the carboy), and another dumping of a bunch of juice
down the drain; using the 8 oz. of juice, really just to put some to use. I had made some ice cubes
from the Meyer lemon juice from our backyard and figured those could add some acidity to the mix;
plus, blackberries and lemon just sound good.

## Log

**3:15pm**. Sanitized Dutch oven. Boil water for 10 mins. Remove from heat and add honey. Stir for 5
mins. Add apple juice and lemon cubes. Keep above 160F for 10mins. Smash up frozen pears and berries
with meat tenderizer and add. Keep above 140F for 15mins. Checked pears on counter and they seemed
just good enough to add; tenderized and added. Wasn’t trying but kept around 150-165F for about
15mins. Ladled some must into grad cylinder, ran underwater for a bit, the fridged to cool for OG
test.

### The Mix

**4:45pm**. OG = `1.104 @ 75F`, corrected to `1.105`. If FG is `1.000`, should end up at
`13.78% ABV`. Covered with foil.

**8:25pm**. Temp at `93F`. the pears I added 2nd are floating and firm. Used the potato masher and
mashed them for around 20 mins, till it was mostly mush. Recovered with foil. Yeast time… 8oz water
in the plastic measuring cup at `100F`. Added yeast and covered with foil. After 15m, yeast looks
moving and settled to the bottom. Added bakers sugar (did not stir) and recovered for 30 mins. Must
still at `84F`. Stirring to cool to `80F` before adding yeast. Added an ice cube to try to cool
faster… No more patience. Added yeast at `82F` and stirred for about 5mins as vigorously as I could
without spilling everywhere. Covered with the same foil as before and left on countertop.

**12am**. Mixed with an egg beater for a minute. Good bubbles.
