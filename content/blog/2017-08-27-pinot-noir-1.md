- Picked at around 5pm.
- Soaked in Kmeta for 30 minutes or so in the sink.
- Ran cold tap water (usually ~78F) over them to rinse for 30-60 minutes (while destemming).
- Plucked each freaking berry by hand. Almost 0 vine included in must (tried to exclude it all).
- Used potato masher to crush.
- Brix ~= 19 (should be 21-25).
- Added 1/4t pectic enzyme and mashed some more.
- Open airlocked (no water) and put by A/C vent in dining room.
