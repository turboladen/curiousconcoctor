## Ingredients

- 36lb sweet Red Tulare cherries (yielded about 2.75G juice)
- 9lb blueberries (yield + pulp = about 1.1G)
- Juice from 2 Granny Smith apples
- Juice from 2 Fuji apples
- Juice from 1 Pink Lady
- Juice from 1 lemon
- 1t. vanilla extract
- 2c water
- 2.5t pectic enzyme
- Handful Scarlet Royal raisins (Fresno State)

(9/2/17: not sure what yeast I used—probably Cotes des Blancs?)

Extracted cherry juice using a steam juicer. Took about 6 hours. Extracted blueberries using
masticating juicer. Added pulp to the must. Total volume is just under 4G. Juiced apples & lemon.
Added vanilla. Added pectic enzyme (dissolved). Added 2c water.
