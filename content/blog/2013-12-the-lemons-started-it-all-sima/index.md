# The Story

It’s winter–New Years Eve Day actually–and I’ve got boxes and boxes of Meyer lemons from our tree in
the backyard. I’ve already made lemon meringue pies, loads of Hot Toddies, and have put lemon in
every thing we can think of to eat or drink. Now what? I thought to myself: “I wonder if I can make
some booze from these!”

We’d tried the fake limoncello once and somehow that went wrong (grew some mold while in the
freezer??), so that was out; it wasn’t really that exiting anyway. So after some short interwebbing,
I came across Sima, the May Day drink of Finland. Sugar + lemons, and a short turn-around time?
Sounded like fun.

I won’t repost the full recipe here, and my version deviated a bit, so check the link above for the
full monty. Instead, I’m posting the ingredients here just to give you an idea of what this is all
about.

## Ingredients

- 4 quarts of water
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 2 lemons, thinly sliced
- 1/8 tsp active dry yeast
- 4 raisins
- 1/8 cup sugar for carbonating

I deviated by…

- Using 5 lemons. Since my Meyers were so sweet and delicious, I figured adding more of these would
  be yummy.
- Using maybe 10 raisins. Cause why not?
- Using Brewer’s Yeast.
