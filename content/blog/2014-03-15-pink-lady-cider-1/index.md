## The Story

A good friend of mine started concocting about the same time I did, yet he decided that in order to
learn characteristics of fermenting that he’d focus primarily on doing things with apples. Seems
like a smart idea: minimize the variables from recipe to recipe, alter a little and see what
results; quite scientific I’d say. So via his concocting experiments, my interest had been further
piqued for doing something with apples.

I recently delved into the cyser world, but I feel like I overcomplicated the recipe there. So many
ingredients. While I think that a lot of flavor can be interesting and fun, I like the idea of
making something simple. A simple recipe should mean simple flavors. Nice and easy. It should also
mean that the next time I use the recipe, since the variable set is small, it will be easy to make
changes for improvements. And part of this whole concocting thing for me is about learning what
ingredients and process do what, then getting better the next time.

In any case, here’s my first attempt at a cider, and a super simple one at that: just fresh, pressed
juice and yeast.

## Recipe

- 5 L juice from [Pink Lady (Cripps Pink)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cripps_Pink) apples
- 1 vial
  [White Labs WLP775 English Cider Yeast](http://www.whitelabs.com/yeast/wlp775-english-cider-yeast)

## Log

### 3/15, 12:19.

OG: `1.052`. Added muslin bag with about 4″ of mash from juicing. Stirred with baster for a couple
mins. Covered with lid and put cheesecloth over airlock hole. Stored in office closet, where temp is
about 72F (yeast directions said to ferment at 70-75).
