- 21 lb. apricots, pitted and frozen
- 4 Pink Lady apples
- 6 Navel oranges
- 1t. almond extract
- 1 vanilla bean
- Lalvin ICV D-47
- ~1t. Pectic enzyme

### 05/16.

Juiced all apricots and put in fermenter. Included skins from about 1/2 of the apricots. Shaved off
peel from 3 oranges and slow boiled for ~10 minutes in water enough to cover the peel. Sliced and
juiced apples and oranges and added to the must.Added some cold water to the orange peel, stirred
and added to the must. At about 3 gallons total for the must at this point. Added water to bring the
must up to 5 gallons. Tasted: seems pretty watery, not lots of apricot flavor. Added the almond
extract, stirred, and tasted again–seemed to bring out more apricot, but still seems a little thin.
Scraped and added vanilla and part of the husk then stirred some more. Finally, added the pectic
enzyme and covered to sit over night.

### 05/17.

Smells much more flavorful today. Pitched yeast.
