Where to start… Mixed while in the Dutch oven and got lots of good bubbles. Tried racking using the
siphon but just got spurts; took forever to get maybe a cupful. Switched to ladle and funnel. Filled
up the carboy and had lots leftover, so I filled up a 750ml flip top. Airlocked the carboy and
ballooned the flippy.

After about 2 mins, both started shooting stuff out the top. Noticed that the carboy was probably
1/3 full of fruit; the flippy was at least half fruit. Seemed like maybe the cap (fruit) was
blocking too much and would do well to use less blocking the CO2 from escaping. Strained out some
juice… Essentially got rid of some fruit to give enough room in the carboy to let it go crazy
bubbling for however long that’s going to last.
