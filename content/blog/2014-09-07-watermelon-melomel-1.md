## Recipe

[Inspiration](http://winemaking.jackkeller.net/watermel.asp)

- 4 seedless watermelons
- juice from 3 lemons
- 2 handfuls raisins
- 1G cotton honey
- 1 packet Red Star Premier Cuvée
- 1 1/4 teaspoon dried pectic enzyme

Used vitamix for first watermelon, then switched to the juicer. Produced 4G juice. Gravity of just
watermelon + lemons: `1.033`. Added honey. Rinsed jar with maybe 2-3cups water. Such mild, yet
interesting flavor. Stirred off and on for 10 mins or so. Forgot to put the honey in while i was
juicing and dumping into the bucket. Added pectic enzyme to 1/4c water. Pitched yeast in 1/4c f 40C
water.

`OG: 1.110`.
