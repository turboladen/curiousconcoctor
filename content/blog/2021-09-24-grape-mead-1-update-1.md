Pressed into brew bucket. About 1.5G yield. Mixed Kirkland wildflower honey (Argentina) with hot
water and blended till dissolved. Mixed into must; at about 2.5G. Added water till just over 3G.
Mixed for a minute or so. Covered and let set for 30 minutes. SG: 1.092 (22 Brix). Pitched Lalvin
