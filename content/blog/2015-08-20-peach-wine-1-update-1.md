Came home from work, opened lid and found a cap! Stirred and got bubbles, so must've been some yeast
already in there. Brix is about 6 SG 1.024, or about 3% ABV if fermented to 1); flavor is real
light. Shouldn't have added so much water. Added date syrup and molasses, although not expecting a
big bump. Gonna roll with the low alcohol here. Pitched the yeast. Moved to the basement, where its
74F.
