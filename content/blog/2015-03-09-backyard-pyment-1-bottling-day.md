5 Bottles. 3 750ml, 2 12oz (well, 1 1/2). Used 9g priming sugar, but forgot to rack onto the sugar,
so just added arbitrarily directly to the bottle. `FG: 1.000`. ABV: `13.39%`

Tastes interesting. Definitely pick up some of the mustiness. The vine-y flavor has dissipated.
