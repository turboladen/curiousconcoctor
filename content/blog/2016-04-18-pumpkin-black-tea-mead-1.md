## Ingredients

- 8 bags Celestial Sweet Harvest Pumpkin tea (black tea, cinnamon, ginger, roasted chicory, pumpkin
  flavor, luo han guo, cloves, nutmeg, stevia extract, pumpkin)
- 8.5 lbs orange honey (Burkett’s Pollination, Reedley)
- 3 lemons: zest from one, juice from all
- handful of raisins
- 3 blackberries (for nutrient)
- Cellar Science Assmanhausen yeast

Seep teabags in hot water (tap) for 10-15 mins. Dumped honey directly into fermenter. added warm
water and raisins, stirred. Let sit for a couple hours (to dissolve honey). Added blackberries.
Rehydrated yeast in 40C water for 20 mins then pitched. Airlocked with no water.

OG: 1.063
