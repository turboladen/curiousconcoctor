## Ingredients

- 3.5G Meyer Lemon juice
- juice from 2 pink lady apples
- 2 handfuls Fresno state princess raisins
- 4lb Wildflower Sage honey (Kristina’s)

Juiced, then added honey, 2t pectic enzyme, raisins.

SG: 1.064
