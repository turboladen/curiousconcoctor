## Ingredients

- 4G Meyer Lemon juice
- 1G palmetto honey (Sweetwater Creek Honey Farm)
- Zest from 3 lemons, soaked in hot-water-tap water for an hour
- 5t Yeast nutrient
- 4t pectic enzyme
- Red Star Premiere Cuvée

Juiced using masticating juicer. Used filter bag. Dissolved honey on stove (low temp); probably 1L
water? Added yeast nutrient and pectic enzyme on day 1. It’s nice and sweet; very little (relative
to lemons) acidity.

SG: 1.112 (~15% potential)
