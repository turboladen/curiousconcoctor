## Recipe

- 14 Murcott Tangerines
- 1 7oz thing of Dried Tart Cherries from WF (minus about 8 :))
- 3 lbs Wildflower Honey from [Kingsburg Honey](http://www.kingsburghoney.com/)
- 1 packet Red Start Première Cuvée

## Log

### 2/15, 1pm.

Washed tangerines in soap and water, lightly scrubbed with the veggie scrubber to get dirt off.
Peeled (no pith!) 8 tangerines (just under 1 cup, loosely packed) and put in cheesecloth sack thing.
Juiced by hand about 8 of them, then juiced the remaining using the juicer (much easier!).

(THERE WAS A PIC HERE)

Filled the fermenter with 2L of water from the fridge. Added the OJ. Added the honey. Added about
3oz of the cherries. Stirred for about 2 mins.

Pitched the yeast in 16oz water at 100F for 15 mins. Stirred the yeast, then dumped into the
fermenter. Stirred the must for a good 4 minutes with an egg beater. Checked OG: 1.102. Bunged and
air-locked. Total liquid is about 5Q.

### 2/16, 10am.

Nice krausen going. Stirred for about 2 mins. pH around 4.0 (maybe a little above?). Tastes really
sweet and yummy.

### 2/16, 8:30pm.

Krausen is back with some good bubbles at the top. Stirred for another 2 mins. No noticeable
bubbling in the airlock.

### 2/17, 9:15pm.

Slight krausen. Audible bubbling with top off. No noticeable bubbling in airlock. Stirred for about
2 mins with egg beater; think I like this better for stirring than the metal serving spoon. Was
concerned about lack of fermenting, so checked SG: ~1.080, so it’s def going. Dumped sample back
into the must.

### 2/19, 6:30pm.

Stirred for 1 min. Removed bag of peels. Tried to beat up the cherries a bit. Took a spoonful;
tastes orangey sweet still, with a bit if bitterness. I want to add more cherries, and don’t wanna
overdo it, so I might wait a bit and just add a small quantity.

### 2/20, 10:30pm.

Pulled lid off for a sec and my nostrils got a sting! Swirled instead of stirring for about 15s.
First noticeable airlock bubbling! …about 1:10.

### 2/23, 7:30pm.

Pulled the lid off, lots of audible bubbling and action. At least an inch of sediment; thinking
there’s probably some pith that made its way into the must and don’t want to risk bitterness from
the pith. Racked to carboy. SG: 1.040. Added the sample back to the must; carboy is almost
completely full for once. After racking, there’s a good amount of bubbling. Drank a small glass:
it’s sweet and nicely and slightly tart–just enough to add some complexity. I really like this
tangerine and honey combo so far.
