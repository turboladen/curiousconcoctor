## Ingredients

- 1 pineapple
- 12 pink lady apples
- 5# wildflower honey (Costco)
- Yeast nutrient
- 3 campden tablets
- Pectic enzyme

Froze the fruit for 2 weeks

Juiced with masticating juicer. Made about 1G juice. Mixed honey with hot water. Added to juice and
added a couple liters of water to get to OG: 1.080. Added yeast nutrient, pectic enzyme, campden
tabs.
