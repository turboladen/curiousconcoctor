More big bubbles in the top middle. Most of the fruit is now gone from the cap. Swirled gently for
about 10s. Notice a bubble or two from the airlock. Smell from the airlock smells sweet and a little
tart.

I’ll wait until the bubbles stop, then rack and add the vanilla. Leave that for a month to clarify
and absorb vanilla. Add oak chips, leave for a week, then bottle.
