Looks like I’ve got about 3.5G of goods to bottle. Gonna use corn sugar for priming. Aiming for just
upper 3’s in vol. CO2, so gonna do 155g of that, mixed in some lukewarm water. Rack the mead onto
it, gently stir, then let sit for about 10 mins–just to make sure it’s dissolved. `FG: 1.035`,
putting ABV (not counting bottle conditioning) at `7.8%`.

5 750ml Champagne bottles 26 12oz bottles

Had at 2 bottles break, 1 wouldn’t take a cap, and another was a screw-on (how’d that get in
there??), so had to grab-and-wash 4 bottles tonight (aka I didn’t sanitize those!).

The high sugar + tart rhubarb = great combo. Should be interesting to see how the carbonation adds
to that.
