Interesting: bubbling about 1:35, but there’s lots of noticeable bubbles inside; looks like
carbonation. Minorly swirled for about 20s, although that didn’t seem to do much for the thick
bottom layer. Tilted at about a 45-degree angle, back and forth, for about 30s. That seemed to cause
a little action in the bottom layer, although that seems to settle back down pretty quickly.
