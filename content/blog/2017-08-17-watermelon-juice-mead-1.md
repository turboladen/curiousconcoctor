## Ingredients

- 1/2G watermelon juice from Costco (Barsotti)
- 2# orange blossom honey (Kristina's)
- Côte des blancs (expired in march)
- 3/4t pectic enzyme
- Juice from 1 small lemon
- 1t yeast nutrient (urea and DAP)

Dissolved honey in hot water. Gravity of juice alone: 1.40
