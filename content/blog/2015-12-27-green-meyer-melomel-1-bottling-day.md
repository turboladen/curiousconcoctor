Was pretty cloudy, overall. Noticed some stringy, yeasty looking stuff throughout the must. Also
noticed some solid, floaty things on top. The 1G jug was about 2/3 full. Used 51g orange blossom
honey (from Minkler), diluted with 1/4c warm water–put that into the brew bucket, poured the must
(yeah, no siphon here) into the bucket and let sit for about 20 mins. There’s a definite extra bit
of junk at the bottom of the carboy, and after leaving the last 1″ or so, I noticed even more
chunky/solid things remaining.

The taste is pretty interesting. I definitely still get that not-quite-ripe flavor–actually more of
that in the nose than on the tongue. It’s quite tart but leaves a pleasant aftertaste, I think.
Amanda’s response was a _cringe_, _pucker_, then a “wow… I like it, but wow…”
