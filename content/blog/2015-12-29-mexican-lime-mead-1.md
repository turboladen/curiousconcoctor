## Ingredients

- 3G lime juice
- 2x1G bags frozen peaches, sliced (probably ~9 lbs)
- 1 handful raisins
- 3t pectic enzyme
- 1G orange blossom honey (Craigslist)
- Côte des blancs yeast

It took 4 hours to juice those damn limes. The kitchen aid juicer broke about 3 hours in.

Diluted the honey in a couple cups of tap water. Heated on low for maybe 4 mins and stirred. Let
cool while I kept juicing.

Juice plus peaches was about 3.5G. Added honey (now up to 4.5G). Added water up to 6G.
