Dissolved in a bit of hot water. Added to Brew Bucket and brought to about 2.5G. Tastes pretty good.

- 1t pectin enzyme
- 2t yeast nutrient
- Lalvin K1-V1116 (pitched at 10:40pm)

Brew bucket lid is on but no air lock; just a towel. OG: 1.054
