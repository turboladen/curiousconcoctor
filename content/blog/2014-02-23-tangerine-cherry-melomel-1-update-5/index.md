### 7:30pm.

Pulled the lid off, lots of audible bubbling and action. At least an inch of sediment; thinking
there’s probably some pith that made its way into the must and don’t want to risk bitterness from
the pith. Racked to carboy. SG: `1.040`.

Added the sample back to the must; carboy is almost completely full for once. After racking, there’s
a good amount of bubbling. Drank a small glass: it’s sweet and nicely and slightly tart–just enough
to add some complexity. I really like this tangerine and honey combo so far.
