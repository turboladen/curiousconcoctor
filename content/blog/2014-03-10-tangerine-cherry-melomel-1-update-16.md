I’m missing the orange flavors and some more body. Juiced the remaining 6 tangerines. Was just
enough juice to fill up the rest of the carboy. Immediate foaming. Probably should’ve strained the
juice as I dumped it in–lots of pulp now. Swirled as best I could off and on for a few mins so the
lees, new juice and pulp got all mixed up; wanted to make sure the juice didn’t just interact with
with yeast at the top. Also added about 10 raisins.
