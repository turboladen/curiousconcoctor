## Recipe

Coming up with a recipe…

- Rhubarb will add acidity ([primarily malic](http://brsquared.org/wine/CalcInfo/FruitInf.htm)).
- Strawberries are fairly [low in tannins](http://brsquared.org/wine/CalcInfo/FruitInf.htm).
- Alcohol level needs to be rather high to balance the tartness.
- Carbonation will balance out lots of body.
- The less tannic, the more acidity can be supported.
- Tannins + acidity should = alcohol.
- Strawberries are relatively low in malic acid
  ([88% of acid in strawberries is citric](http://www.rsc.org/education/eic/issues/2012May/whats-in-your-strawberries.asp)),
  so no need to pick a yeast that targets malic acid (ex. 71b-1122).
- Fermenting to dry will add to the amount of tartness, so it’ll be good to leave some sweetness

Use [this recipe for Strawberry Rhubarb Wine](http://winemaking.jackkeller.net/request165.asp) as a
reference. 12lbs strawberries, frozen for ~2 months. 2.5lbs rhubarb, frozen for ~2 months. Use 1G
raspberry honey from Heritage Honey.

### 7/12/15

Juiced all strawberries–added both juice and pulp to the must. Totaled ~2G (just under). SG of
strawberries alone: `1.033`. Added juice of 2 small organic lemons. Added rhubarb to stainless steel
bowl, mixed in 1c sugar and let sit overnight (~10 hours).

### 7/13/15 7am.

Mixed in the rhubarb mixture and 3t pectic enzyme. Stirred. Looks about 6G now.

### 9pm.

`OG: 1.095`. Pitched Cotes des Blancs.
