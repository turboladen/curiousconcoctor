### 13:00.

Racked, new airlock. `SG: 1.022`, which is concerning–only dropped 0.003 in a week. `pH ~= 3.7`?
Aroma… Can smell honey and tangerine. Taste… Much tarter than you’d expect from the smell, which
covers up the residual sugar for sure. Mouth… A little thicker than I’d like, but certainly
attributable to the residual sugar.

### 23:00.

Added some vanilla extract to my glass. It was pretty good, but seemed to compete with the cherry
tartness. Gonna skip vanilla this time.
