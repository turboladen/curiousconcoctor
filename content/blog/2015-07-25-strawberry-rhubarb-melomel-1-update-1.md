Used the refractometer to get a reading: `SG = 1.035`. Fermenting less quickly than I anticipated,
but could also be stuck (wondering if it got too hot in here while we were on vacation last
weekend). If it is stuck, I’m more curious than concerned and might just ride it out as is instead
of trying to get it going again.

Flavor-wise, the rhubarb definitely came out since my last stir. It’s wonderfully tart! And still
retaining good strawberry flavor.
