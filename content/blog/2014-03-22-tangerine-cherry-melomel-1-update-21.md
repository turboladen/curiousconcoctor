Still bubbling, although slowly. About 3/4” sediment—looks pretty settled/packed (maybe indicative
of getting close to being done with floccing?)—and maybe a teaspoon of pulp floating towards the
top. Racked. SG: 1.004. Good flavor balance now actually. A little harsh still; aging will be good.
Liquid level is at the top of the “O” in “One gallon” on the side of the carboy. Can definitely
smell the honey.
