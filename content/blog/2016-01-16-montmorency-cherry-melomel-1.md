Some say to use 71b-1122, lots say to use a champagne yeast, and other say D47. This, however, rings
true with me, so I’m gonna go with Montrachet. This says temps for Montrachet are 59-86, so with the
basement hanging around 61-62, it should be safe to keep down there until it warms up. I also like
the idea of retaining some of the tartness—that’s totally up my alley. Plus I have a bunch of that
yeast to use up. :)

## Ingredients

- 5 oranges (from Jeanette)
- 3 vanilla beans
- 2.5t pectic enzyme
- 1 bottle Montmorency cherry concentrate
- pits from about 2lb Bing cherries (frozen)
- ~5G water
- 1G Sage honey (from madera guy)
- 1 packet Red Star Montrachet yeast

Empty concentrate into brew bucket. Start filling with water. Add pectic enzyme while filling, so it
gets mixed in. Add zest from 2 of the oranges. Add juice from all 5. Cut, scrape and add vanilla
beans and husk (will remove the husks after primary). Add cherry pits. Add honey. Stir, stir stir.

OG: 1.092. Seems kinda low. I’ll check again later. Checked a couple hours later and was the same
