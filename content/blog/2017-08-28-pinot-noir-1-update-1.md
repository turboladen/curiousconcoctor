Acid test kit shows:

- % tartaric: .1875 (guide says it should be between .60 and .65)
- ppt sulfuric: 1.2 (guide says it should be between 3.9 and 4.2)

Added 4.5oz cane sugar to bring brix up to 22.

did acid test again:

- % TA: .40
- ppt sulfuric: 2.56

Added 1.5t tartaric acid to bring TA up to .625.
