Removed muslin bag. Swirled for 30s. `SG: 1.032`. Noticeable bottom sediment. Left raisins in.
Smells a little alcoholy, although apples and spice aromas evident. Taste… Not tons of zing at this
point–expected more than this. The flavors aren’t quite all there… Or maybe it’s just that no one
flavor is really that distinct. This may change with more alcohol. Not sure yet if tannins from
oaking would benefit. Aftertaste is definitely apple (after maybe 8 sips over the past 5 mins); nice
lingering flavor. I just realized I'm not sure I can taste any honey, which I’m totally OK with.
