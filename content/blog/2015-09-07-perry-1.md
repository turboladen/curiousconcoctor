## Ingredients

- 25.3# dark skinned pear (Bosc?)
- 20# Bartlett
- Handful raisins
- 3 vanilla beans
- 2 Ceylon cinnamon sticks (one ground)
- Cote des blanc yeast

Juiced almost 1 bag of each. Each turned to sauce with almost 0 pulp. Dumped the remains in whole.
Next bag I smashed with a potato masher. Separated the juice alright but only yields like a cup or
two from 5lbs. 😱

Pitched yeast. OG: 1.054.

Added 1 vanilla bean, cinnamon, raisins. Tastes like it could use some acid, maybe citric?
