- 4 still 750s

The rest carbonated. Aiming for 5.5 volumes, so 74g corn sugar. Ended up with 4 750s and 1 375.
Decided to pitch some Red Star Premier cuvée and add that in. While I’m at it, I’m kinda thinking
about doing méthode champagnoise...
<https://brewerylane.com/wine/sparkling-wine-champagne-method.html>

It actually tastes fairly well balanced for a first try. A little too juicy on the front and too
yeasty smelling, but the rest is ok. Hopefully that plays well into the champagne. 🤞
