## Recipe

- 8 lbs cherries
- 4 vanilla beans
- 2c white sugar
- 2c brown sugar
- 2c maple syrup
- 5 vanilla beans
- 1/4t pectic enzyme

Doing 1G fermented, 1G not fermented.

Added cherries to dutch oven plus 5c water (fridge filtered), put on medium-low heat. Added all
sugars and stirred. Scraped all vanilla beans, added; added 3 husks. Let temp get up to 160F, let
stay for about 5-10 mins, then took off, covered and let cool. Ladelled equally into two separate 1G
carboys. Mixed the pectic enzyme with a couple tablespoons of cool water (to dissolve), then added
equally to each carboy.

Let sit overnight (pectic enzyme). Added 4c vodka to the no-ferment carboy.
