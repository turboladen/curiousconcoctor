5 bottles: 2 750ml, 2 375ml, 1 12oz (almost full). Racked onto 18g priming sugar.

Taste. Lots of sweetness has dissipated, but it doesn’t seem so dry that it’s off-putting. Lots of
the lavender has dissipated too, but not it’s not overpowering; seems like a good level. Kinda
thinking again that this might be just as good if not better still.
